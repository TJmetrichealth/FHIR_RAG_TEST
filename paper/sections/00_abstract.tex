\begin{abstract}
Clinical records are increasingly available both as structured HL7 FHIR R4B resources and
as LLM-generated narrative summaries.  When both encode the same facts, it is unclear which
should serve as the retrieval substrate for downstream question answering (QA).  We present
a paired-data comparison in which 200 synthetic specialty-regimen patients are rendered as
FHIR~R4B bundles and as narratives generated from the same bundle, and three
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems answer the same 13,800 programmatically
verified adherence-indicator questions across five Patient Support Program (PSP) question
families and three regimen-complexity tiers.  Two further arms (a no-retrieval baseline
and a deterministic templated-narrative source) isolate the contribution of retrieval and
of LLM-specific narrative style.

\textbf{Headline result and what it is made of.}  On aggregate exact-match accuracy the
narrative arm (System~A, 40.6\%) leads the naive structured arm (System~B, 35.3\%) and the
resource-aware structured arm (System~C, 33.4\%); all pairwise gaps survive a patient-level
cluster bootstrap.  Three post-hoc analyses show that this gap is not a representation
effect.  (1)~\emph{Abstention.}  40.7\% of ground-truth answers are N/A because the
queried component does not exist for that patient; a constant ``always N/A'' predictor
scores 40.7\%, matching System~A.  Under the pre-specified scorer, the arms score 76.4\%,
64.7\% and 59.2\% on N/A questions and 16.0\%, 15.1\% and 15.6\% on the 8,177 questions
with a substantive answer, so the entire aggregate lead is differential abstention, and the
previously reported Tier-3 ``collapse'' is the fall in N/A prevalence from 54--59\% to
13.8\%.  (2)~\emph{Scorer artefact.}  The scorer reads the answer LLM's reasoning trace;
re-scoring only the final answer raises System~A's substantive accuracy to 28.7\% while
leaving B and C at 16.8\% and 17.7\%, so the substantive-question result is
scorer-dependent.  (3)~\emph{Summary leakage.}  The narrative advantage that remains under
final-answer scoring is concentrated in the families whose answers the narrative
generation prompt writes into the text (last and first administration dates, total
administration counts), and is absent on the family both representations state equally,
consistent with the narrative being a curated summary rather than an alternative format
of equal content.

\textbf{Design flaws disclosed.}  The question bank refers to a ``reference date'' that
was never passed to any system, making 7,800 questions unanswerable as posed; the answer
LLM (Qwen3-32B) ran in thinking mode under a 512-token output cap and 31--38\% of
responses were truncated inside the reasoning block; and the scorer's free-text path
credits no correct answers on the missed-dose family.  We quantify each from the released
raw outputs, report N/A-stratified and cluster-robust statistics throughout, and state
which conclusions survive.  The feature-extraction arm is retained as an exploratory
supplement.

Code (Apache-2.0) and the synthetic dataset (CC-BY-4.0) are released as a combined Zenodo
deposit at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20292792}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.20292792}.
\end{abstract}
