\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Synthetic data only (R1 mitigation context).}
All 200 patients are generated by Synthea with a custom specialty-regimen overlay.
Synthea's medication module is known to deviate from real prescribing
distributions~\citep{hodges2023medication} and models only guideline-concordant
pathways without real-world deviations~\citep{meeker2022synthea}.
Our overlay addresses the specialty-medication gap (R8 mitigation) but cannot
introduce the messy, noisy, protocol-discordant adherence patterns present in real
PSP data.  Absolute accuracy numbers should not be extrapolated to production
clinical workflows.  External validity to real de-identified PSP data is the explicit
follow-up step (metricHEALTH Phase 3, post-REB).

\paragraph{ANSWER\_MAX\_TOKENS = 512 is a dominant confounder (R4 artefact, materialised).}
The 512-token output budget (set to control API costs during the evaluation run)
is the dominant failure cause across all systems (44--58\% of sampled failures are
reasoning-truncated; Table~\ref{tab:errors}).  This artefact disproportionately
penalises structured systems whose FHIR-JSON contexts require longer chain-of-thought
arithmetic.  The A $>$ B $>$ C ordering should therefore be interpreted as conditional
on this budget constraint, not as a general architectural property of narrative vs.\
structured RAG.  Future work should sweep ANSWER\_MAX\_TOKENS to isolate the
architecture effect.  The sensitivity sweep listed as future work (decision C2 in
\texttt{docs/decisions.md}) was not completed within the Week~3 timeline.

\paragraph{Single answer LLM and single embedding model.}
Results are conditioned on \texttt{qwen/qwen3-32b} (Groq) as the answer LLM and
\texttt{BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5} as the embedding model.  Robustness across other
LLMs or domain-adapted embeddings (MedCPT~\citep{jin2023medcpt}) is flagged as future
work.  The templated-narrative ablation (deterministic generator, no LLM) was generated and
passed the fidelity audit at 100\% entity recall across all 200 patients and all entity
classes (O1 gate: PASS; same result as the LLM narrative arm).  A downstream QA
evaluation against the templated narratives was planned to test robustness of the
A $>$ B $>$ C ordering to narrative-LLM choice, but that evaluation was not completed
within the results-freeze timeline.  Whether structured systems would beat or tie
templated narratives remains an open question, deferred to the full venue version.

\paragraph{Small per-cell N at tier $\times$ family intersections.}
With 200 patients across 3 tiers and 5 families, the per-cell question counts
range from approximately 250 to 350.  Wilson CIs at those cell sizes are approximately
$\pm$3--5~pp; comparisons within cells should be treated as exploratory.

\paragraph{Single-rater error taxonomy (decision C3, materialised).}
The 150-failure error taxonomy (50 per system) was coded by a single rater (TJ)
using deterministic regex rules; no LLM-based triangulation was performed --- the taxonomy is fully deterministic and reproducible from the regex rules in \texttt{analysis/run\_error\_taxonomy.py}.  An ``Other / unclassified'' residual of 4--8\% indicates that the
five-category taxonomy (four primary plus residual) does not exhaust all failure modes.  No formal inter-rater
reliability coefficient is reported because the design is single-rater by intent
(decision C3).

\paragraph{Conservative v1 retrieval recall heuristic.}
Recall@k for Systems~B and~C is computed using a heuristic that checks whether the
top-$k$ chunk IDs include the patient's primary MedicationRequest or specialty CarePlan.
This is a conservative proxy; a full per-question source-resource mapping (requiring
ground-truth functions in ``explain'' mode) is deferred to a follow-up release.

\paragraph{Feature-extraction arm: synthetic adherence label.}
The FS-Structured AUC near 1.0 reflects label/feature alignment in the synthetic design:
all positive cases are Tier-2 patients, and FS-Structured directly encodes tier as a
one-hot feature.  The near-perfect AUC should not be interpreted as evidence that
structured features will achieve similar performance on real, noisy adherence outcomes.
The feature-extraction arm is a design-informing benchmark, not a production predictor
(decision G3 scope; risk R15 acknowledged).

\paragraph{Groq Developer paid plan disclosure.}
The full evaluation matrix used the Groq Developer paid plan, authorised 2026-05-05 with
a \$20~USD ceiling (decision log 2026-05-05).  Observed total API cost was \$32.68
(System A: \$4.74; B: \$21.07; C: \$6.88), modestly exceeding the single-system
ceiling for B.  All calls used temperature~0.0; answers are cached and reproducible.
The data is fully synthetic; no PHI was transmitted to the API.

\paragraph{Three tiers may not cover all real-world regimen complexity.}
The three complexity tiers are designed to span simple LAI, cyclic, and multi-drug
regimens, but do not cover all specialty-medication scenarios (infusion-only regimens,
combination-biologic prescribing, variable-interval dosing).  Adherence metrics
(PDC, MPR) are implemented to standard definitions~\citep{lociganic2015using} but edge
cases (overlapping refills, dose reductions, bridge therapies) are not represented in
the synthetic dataset.
