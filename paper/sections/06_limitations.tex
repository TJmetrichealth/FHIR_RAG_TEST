\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Benchmark construction: missing reference date.}
The question strings refer to a reference date that was not supplied to any system
(\S\ref{sec:results:flaws}, Defect~1).  7,800 questions are affected.  Substantive
accuracy on the windowed-count, ratio, and comparator templates is therefore not a
measure of what the systems could do with a well-posed question, and the
\texttt{regimen\_compliance} and \texttt{temporal\_comparison} substantive rows should
not be interpreted as representation results.  Fixing this requires regenerating the
question strings with the date interpolated and rerunning all arms.

\paragraph{Answer LLM ran in thinking mode under a tight cap.}
Qwen3-32B was run with Groq's default reasoning behaviour and a 512-token output budget
shared between reasoning and answer; 30.6--38.2\% of retrieval-arm responses were
truncated before a final answer (Defect~2).  The 1024-token sweep on 495 questions reduces
but does not remove truncation and does not change substantive accuracy.  We did not rerun
the full matrix with thinking disabled or with a multi-thousand-token budget, and any
claim about the answer model's ability on this task is conditional on this configuration.

\paragraph{Scorer behaviour.}
The scorer extracts the first date or number from the full response, including the
reasoning trace, and its free-text path credits no correct comparator or component-name
answers (Defect~3).  The final-answer re-scoring in \S\ref{sec:results:flaws} shows that
the first issue is not neutral: it cost the narrative arm about 13~pp of substantive
accuracy and the structured arms about 2~pp, so the pre-specified substantive tie and
the final-answer narrative lead are both scorer-conditional.  Neither scoring is
obviously correct.  Final-answer scoring is what a deployed system would be judged on, but
it awards zero to every truncated response and so entangles scoring with Defect~2.  The
second issue means one family's substantive accuracy is unmeasured under either scoring.
A corrected scorer that parses only the final answer, matches categorical ground-truth
tokens, and is run on untruncated responses is required before absolute accuracy numbers
should be compared across papers.

\paragraph{The pairing does not hold information content constant.}
The narrative is a curated overlay summary with a precomputed administration count and
at most seven dates per component; the bundle is a complete record with every
administration and a large irrelevant Synthea base.  The narrative also fits in one
chunk, so the narrative arm performs no retrieval.  The comparison is between rendering
pipelines, not between representations of matched content (\S\ref{sec:discussion}).
The three arms that would isolate representation (overlay-only structured, compact
structured, aggregate-stripped narrative) have not been run.

\paragraph{No tool-calling or query baseline.}
Because ground truth is programmatic, a SQL-on-FHIR or function-calling arm would score
near ceiling.  We argue in \S\ref{sec:discussion} that this does not answer the
free-text substrate question, but we have not demonstrated that free-text QA is the
appropriate deployment setting for count and ratio questions, and we suspect it is not.

\paragraph{Post-hoc analyses.}
The N/A stratification, the constant-N/A baseline, the final-answer re-scoring and the
cluster bootstrap were not pre-specified; they were introduced in response to review and
to our own audit.  The pre-specified analysis is the question-level one in
Table~\ref{tab:pairwise} under the original scorer, and we have kept it visible.  The
summary-leakage interpretation of the final-answer family pattern is a reading of
observational data, not a tested hypothesis.

\paragraph{Synthetic data only.}
All 200 patients are generated by Synthea with a custom specialty-regimen overlay.
Synthea's medication module is known to deviate from real prescribing
distributions~\citep{hodges2023medication} and models only guideline-concordant
pathways~\citep{meeker2022synthea}.  The overlay is perfectly scheduled, which is why
the feature-arm label has positives only at Tier~2.  Absolute accuracy numbers should not
be extrapolated to production workflows.

\paragraph{Single answer LLM and single embedding model.}
Results are conditioned on \path{qwen/qwen3-32b} as the answer LLM and
\path{BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5} as the embedding model.  The templated arm shows the
narrative-side result does not depend on the narrative-generating LLM; nothing here
tests robustness to the answer LLM, and a model with different abstention behaviour
under the same prompt could change the N/A results materially.

\paragraph{Exploratory feature arm.}
The synthetic label is a function of features that FS-Structured contains almost
verbatim, all positives are Tier-2, and the three feature sets differ in engineering
effort.  The arm shows that the release supports a reproducible downstream task; it does
not support a representation claim (\S\ref{sec:results:o6}).

\paragraph{Conservative retrieval recall heuristic.}
Recall@k for Systems~B and~C checks whether the top-$k$ chunks include the patient's
primary MedicationRequest or specialty CarePlan.  It is a proxy for retrieving ``the''
supporting resource and does not verify that the specific resource needed for a given
question was retrieved.

\paragraph{Per-cell sample sizes.}
With 200 patients across 3 tiers and 5 families, per-cell question counts range from
504 to 1{,}554, and the substantive subsets are smaller still (for example 252 N/A
\texttt{cross\_resource} questions).  Comparisons within cells should be treated as
exploratory, and the cluster bootstrap is the appropriate interval for any of them.

\paragraph{Three tiers do not cover all regimen complexity.}
Infusion-only regimens, combination-biologic prescribing, variable-interval dosing,
overlapping refills, dose reductions, and bridge therapies are not represented.
Adherence metrics are implemented to standard definitions~\citep{lociganic2015using}.
